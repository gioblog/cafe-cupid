# Cafe Cupid 
To make running the local cafe a little more exciting, you, the cafe owner, decide to dabble in love lives of your regular customers. Cafe Cupid is a romance sim visual novel that allows you to play matchmaker with all the characters you meet. Watch the drama unfold as you influence the decisions of your customers through dialogue options and how you make their drinks. Pair the right customers together to unlock the true ending or try out other combinations for a different ending! 

This project was started on July 17th, 2026 as part of my participation in the Hack Your Summer program.
